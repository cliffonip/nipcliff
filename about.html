About CLIFF

The official IP awareness community making creativity bankable.

Who We Are

CLIFF — the Creative Law and Intellectual Property Finance Forum — is the official Intellectual Property awareness community created to educate, empower, and connect creatives, innovators, and professionals.

We exist to help you understand and protect your Intellectual Property, monetise it as a financial asset, navigate legal and business structures around creativity, and access funding opportunities using IP.

CLIFF is committed to building a system where creativity is not just appreciated — but bankable. Whether you're a musician, filmmaker, lawyer, or investor, this is your community.

Our Mission

To educate, empower, and connect creatives, innovators, and professionals on how to understand, protect, and monetise their Intellectual Property — and to build a system where creativity is not just appreciated, but bankable.

Our Vision

A Nigeria — and Africa — where every creative professional understands the value of their Intellectual Property, can protect it effectively, and can access financial opportunities through it.

Our Four Pillars

🎯

Understand & Protect

Helping creatives understand their IP rights and the legal frameworks that protect their work.

💰

Monetise IP

Providing strategies and structures to turn intellectual property into a real, tangible financial asset.

⚖️

Navigate Legal Structures

Demystifying the legal and business frameworks around creativity so you can operate with confidence.

🏦

Access Funding

Creating structured pathways for creatives to unlock funding and investment using their IP.
