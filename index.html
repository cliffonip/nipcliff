About CLIFF

The official Intellectual Property awareness community of the Creative Law and Intellectual Property Finance Forum.

CLIFF was created to educate, empower, and connect creatives, innovators, and professionals on how to understand and protect their Intellectual Property — and more importantly, how to monetise it as a financial asset.

We are committed to building a system where creativity is not just appreciated — but bankable. From musicians to lawyers, filmmakers to investors, CLIFF bridges the gap between creative work and financial opportunity.
